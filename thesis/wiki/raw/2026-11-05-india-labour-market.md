# India labour market data (Nov 2026)

Headline: overall unemployment 5.0% (Sep 2026), LFPR 55.5% — looks resilient.

The real story (freshers/graduates):
- Youth (15–29) unemployment: 16.2% (Jun 2026) — up from 13.8% (Apr 2025).
- Educated (secondary+) unemployment: 6.5% (2025, improved from 7.0% in 2024).
- Graduate unemployment: male 19.2%, female 29.4% — actively seeking, not disengaged.
- 67% of unemployed youth are now graduates (~11M degree-holders aged 20–29 jobless).
- ~85% of engineering graduates unplaced in 2026 despite active hiring (talent report).
- 44% of graduates not job-ready; 80% of companies can't find the right talent despite vacancies.
- Commanding Heights framing: 16 net tech jobs per 100 graduates (Nasscom FY2026); TCS headcount -23,460 vs op profit +7.5%.
- Stanford study: employment of 22–25 year-olds in AI-exposed occupations -19% vs peers (mid-2026); experienced workers unaffected (hiring freeze, not firings).

Interpretation: not "no jobs" — a brutal skills mismatch with the entry-level cognitive rung being automated out first. The Intelligence Inversion hitting India's fresher pipeline.
