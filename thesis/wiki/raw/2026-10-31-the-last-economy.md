# The Last Economy (digest)

Thesis: the "Intelligence Inversion" — human intellect flips from the scarcest, most expensive input in the economy to the cheapest and most abundant, within ~1,000 days. Work, money and meaning crack apart because they were built on scarce intelligence.

Key elements:
- 1,000-day window; three futures: Digital Feudalism (few labs own the Oracle AIs), Great Fragmentation, or open/decentralised intelligence as infrastructure ("like water").
- Any job performed on a screen: AI does it "better, faster, cheaper" in the near future.
- The "second economy": a machine-speed economy running faster than the human one; firms and markets reframed as information-processing systems.
- New social contract based on dignity, capability, viability: universal access to intelligence as a baseline right.
- Seven Fatal Lies chapter: dismantles standard comfort narratives.
- Open-source AI framed as a stability requirement, not ideology — the antidote to Digital Feudalism.
- Humans' residual roles: owning, verifying, caring — accountability, trust, physical presence.
