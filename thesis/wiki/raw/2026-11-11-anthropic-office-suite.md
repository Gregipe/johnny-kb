# Anthropic office suite (digest)

Announced Nov 10-11, 2026 at an SF event, ahead of the IPO:

- New tools: Claude Meetings (joins meetings, takes notes; Teams/Enterprise), Claude Sheets (edit spreadsheets alongside users), Claude Whiteboards (shared idea layout) — beta. Claude Data (ex-Dashboards, metrics analysis) now GA on all paid plans.
- Joins existing Claude Docs, Slides, Design, Motion. Presentation → 1-min explainer video.
- Interactive connectors: HubSpot, Adobe PDF editor, Atlassian etc. embedded INSIDE Claude — no tab switching. MCP marketplace now 9,000+ plugins.
- Positioning (Nicholas Lin): Claude Code "changed how engineers work a year ago"; now closing the gap for non-developers: "Now we want to expand that reach toward everybody else."
- Context: IPO pressure — needs revenue + engagement; courting white-collar workers.
- Competitors: Google Gemini (meetings, Sheets), OpenAI ChatGPT record + Dots agents with connectors.
