# Financial-freedom anti-guru essay (digest)

Thesis: financial freedom is misrepresented by gurus selling fake lifestyles; the real path is simple, hard, and behavioural. Core beats:

- "Easy is a lie. Passive income is a lie." Anything that looks easy is a scam.
- Nail your why first — family is the cheat code; without a strong why, hard things get abandoned.
- Pick a financial-freedom number and accept it will move; stop chasing the comparison treadmill.
- Part-time moonshots: dream big, but never stake everything on the lottery bet.
- Investing-as-gambling critique; stop following influencers.
- The path is owning a business, aligned with skills, obsessions, and desired lifestyle.
- Free your calendar — busy is a choice; the same 24 hours for everyone.
- Insane amounts of action: 200 DMs, not one; thousands of essays.
- The whole model: add value daily until money follows. No shortcut.
