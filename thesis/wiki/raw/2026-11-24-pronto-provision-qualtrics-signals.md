## Digest

1. Pronto → Provision Labs: founder Anjali Sardana (Pronto = vernacular content → WhatsApp business-engagement SaaS) pivots the company into "physical AI"; early talks to raise $40-50M with G Squared, Founders Fund, a16z; Pronto's existing investors become Provision shareholders; Pronto continues as subsidiary. 2. JPMorgan hung deal: the $6.75B Qualtrics purchase of Press Ganey Forsta was financed with a $5.3B package (JPMorgan-led; Goldman, Morgan Stanley, UBS + 8 others in bridge). In March, the syndication stalled — investors objected to Qualtrics' exposure to the software selloff and AI disruption fears. Biggest hung deal of 2026; bank group >$500M paper losses; now trying to place ~$2B HY bonds at 85 cents (~9.4% yield). Dimon (Oct 13 call): "We are tightening up credit a little bit. I think some of the credit terms have gotten a little bit loose." A clean sale = signal stuck debt can clear; a weak one = banks slow to underwrite the next software buyout.

## Joint hypothesis

The two stories are the same story seen from the founder side and the credit side: AI is repricing the software layer. Equity and founder option value rotate INTO physical AI (embodiment, atoms, delivery); credit markets mark DOWN pure-software cash flows as AI-exposed. Software — especially engagement/survey SaaS — moved from scarce asset to collateral damage. The premium sits in the physical/trust/delivery layer.
