# Concept: K-Shaped Economy

**Definition:** The divergence where owners of scarce assets compound wealth while wage-earners — especially the newly-displaced cognitive-labour class — stagnate or fall. "A K opens at every level": internationally, within nations, within firms, within individuals.

**Provenance:**
- The Commanding Heights (Part V: The World to Come). Source: `sources/commanding-heights`.
- Reinforced by India jobs data (`concepts/india-labour-market`) and the anti-guru essay's audience framing (`concepts/anti-guru-positioning`).

**Core claims:**
1. First act (monetary): owners split from earners. Second act (technological): owners split from owners (2026).
2. India's educated aspirant class is the epicentre of the bottom-K; emigration paths are closing (2026).
3. "Towers of Ivory": concierge medicine, tutors, elite schools, private security — fixed-supply demand at the K-top (2026).
4. Traditional metrics (GDP, employment stats) misread the K; watch vendor headcount disclosures and Gulfstream backlogs instead (2026).
