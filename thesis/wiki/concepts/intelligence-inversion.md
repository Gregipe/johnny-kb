# Concept: Intelligence Inversion

**Definition:** The phase change where human intellect — historically the scarcest, most expensive economic input — becomes the cheapest and most abundant one, restructuring work, money and meaning. Mostaque's term; ~1,000-day horizon.

**Provenance:**
- Emad Mostaque (`entities/emad-mostaque`), "The Last Economy" (2026). Source: `sources/the-last-economy`.

**Core claims:**
1. Screen-bound cognitive labour is absorbed first; AI does it better, faster, cheaper (2026).
2. Humans' durable roles: owning, verifying, caring — accountability and presence (2026).
3. Value concentrates in whoever owns the leveraged position; the squeezed middle is the former cognitive-labour class (2026).
4. Open/decentralised intelligence is a stability play against Digital Feudalism (2026).
