# Concept: India Labour Market Crisis

**Definition:** The structural failure of India's job market to absorb its freshers and graduates — a skills-mismatch crisis where entry-level cognitive work is being automated out from under the newest cohort before they start.

**Provenance:**
- Greg's voice note (2026-11-05) + PLFS 2025 data. Source: `sources/india-labour-market`.
- Foreshadowed in The Commanding Heights (Ravi narrative; 16 jobs per 100 graduates).

**Core claims:**
1. Headline unemployment (~5%) masks graduate unemployment of 19–29% (PLFS 2025).
2. It's a mismatch, not a shortage: ~85% of engineering grads unplaced while 80% of employers report unfillable vacancies (2026).
3. The first rung is being pulled up: young workers are not hired (-19% vs peers), not fired (Stanford, mid-2026).
4. Emigration paths — the historical safety valve — are closing (`sources/commanding-heights`, Part V).

**Why it matters to Greg:**
- Validates his fresher instinct with citable data.
- The displaced aspirant cohort is the audience for the anti-guru/ownership path (`concepts/anti-guru-positioning`).
- Signals the market size and urgency behind financial-independence tools for India.

**Contradictions/notes:**
- PLFS overall UR fell 2024→2025; the crisis is concentrated in educated youth — both facts true simultaneously. Don't cite one without the other.
