# Concept: Commanding Heights

**Definition:** Scarce, controllable positions of economic power whose holders durably capture the gains of increasingly capable and diffused intelligence. The crossings where value pools as intelligence commoditises. From Lenin's 1922 defence of the NEP: "We hold all the Commanding Heights."

**Provenance:**
- Coined by Lenin (1922); reframed for the AI era by Will Manidis & Joe Roberts, commanding-heights.com (2026). Source: `sources/commanding-heights`.

**Core claims:**
1. Intelligence is the giveaway/loss leader; margins accrue to the conduit — "one either owns the rails or pays those who do" (2026).
2. Durability test: scarce + controllable + entrenched by the activity it enables. Fleeting bottlenecks (power → transmission → chips) migrate; Heights endure (2026).
3. Distribution and network ownership are the durable Heights of the intelligence era (2026).
4. Most firms are coal for the furnace — data businesses should be sold, not held (2026).
5. The sovereign meters access to central technologies (antitrust as the map of toll accumulation) (2026).
