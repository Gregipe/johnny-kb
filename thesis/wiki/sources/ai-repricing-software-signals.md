1. Pronto → Provision Labs (Moneycontrol, 2026-11-24): Anjali Sardana pivots from WhatsApp business-engagement SaaS to physical AI; raising $40-50M (G Squared, Founders Fund, a16z engaged); Pronto becomes a Provision Labs subsidiary.
2. ## Claim (synthesis, dated 2026-11-24)

AI's repricing wave has moved from equity into credit, and from digital into physical. Founder option value and venture capital rotate into embodied/physical AI while credit markets mark down pure-software cash flows as AI-exposed. Software — engagement and survey SaaS in particular — has flipped from scarce asset to collateral damage; the premium sits in the physical/trust/delivery layer.

## Connections

- Direct market confirmation of Greg's moat framework: no pure-software moats; trust, distribution, data, regulatory permission, physical presence, practitioner accountability. Both sides of the market (founders + credit) are now pricing exactly that.
- Warning signal for Hunar's packaging: Pronto was literally a WhatsApp engagement SaaS. Its founder exiting software engagement for physical AI says engagement-software is commoditising; value must live in accountable delivery (outcomes, regulated workflows, physical onboarding), not in the software tool.
- Complements the Applied Compute/Palo Alto datapoint: capital pays premium for the crossing (trust, security, delivery) while the code layer commoditises.
- Complements intelligence-inversion: when intelligence gets abundant, the scarce asset is the physical/trust layer — the repricing is now observable in debt markets, not just equity.
