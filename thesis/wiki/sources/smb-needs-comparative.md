## Core finding
The anxiety mix rotated: 2024 was supply-side (credit, payments-rail continuity, retrospective GST demands); 2026 is demand- and security-side (quick-commerce cannibalisation, industrial-scale fraud, new per-transaction digital costs).

## Amplified 2024→2026
- Quick-commerce displacement of kirana demand (67% kirana owners report lower sales, Datum 2024; market ~$6B→$11.5B; 6,000+ dark stores by Apr 2026; zero restrictive regulation)
- Cyber-fraud / digital-arrest fear (cases 60,676→1,23,672; UPI fraud losses ₹573cr→₹1,100cr)
- Labour-code payroll shock (four codes effective 21 Nov 2025; EPFO ceiling ₹15k→₹25k from 17 Sep 2026)
- System-triggered GST mismatch notices (Rule 88C/88D, 7-day windows; notices +33% to ~2 lakh in FY25)
- Delayed-payments receivables squeeze (~₹20,458 cr pending in MSME Samadhaan as of Oct 2026)

## Cooled
- Coercive GST edge (rate simplification Sept 2025; arrest powers withdrawn, prosecution threshold ₹1cr→₹5cr, penalty −60%, 57th Council Oct 2026)
- ONDC must-adopt anxiety (retail orders 6.5M→~3.7M, unpublished since Sept 2025; pivot to logistics/B2B)

## Emerging 2025-26
- End of free UPI (NPCI 0.4% merchant fee on >₹2,000, Sept 2026, deferred after CAIT objections)
- AI capability gap (84% of Indian/APAC SMBs use AI; 86% need more training; 46.2% of MSMEs fully offline)

## Integrity notes
- Two unverifiable items ("GST SAHAJ 2.0", "IBC Rule 158B") excluded rather than fabricated.
- Fraud-loss datasets conflict by an order of magnitude (I4C vs ORF/NHRC) — flagged in report.
- AICPDF's "2 lakh kirana closures" is a lobbyist estimate, marked unverified.
