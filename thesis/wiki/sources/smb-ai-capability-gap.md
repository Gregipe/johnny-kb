**Produced by:** Johnny (delegated research), for Greg — general research
**Date:** 2026-11-18

## Core finding
The dominant AI need-statement is demand-side: "help me get found, chosen, and answered by customers." Top actual uses: content creation, advertising, customer communication — NOT accounting/inventory/compliance. Adoption broad (65.8-84%) but shallow (only 5% have AI core to operations; 25% integrated into workflows).

## The help-gap ranking
1. Skills + hands-on implementation — setting up/navigating tools is the hardest challenge (36.8% ISF/Meta, n=7,835) vs 5-8% for finance/hiring/customers
2. Cost and ROI clarity — 51% cite cost as top barrier to AI training; most-requested support is clearer cost/ROI info
3. Data/system readiness — 65.8% use AI but only 21% have any ERP; half can't measure ROI; AI leapfrogs basic digitisation (runs as chat over no system of record)

## Surprising data point
65.8% of Indian MSMEs use AI tools vs 21% using ANY ERP — AI has leapfrogged digitisation.

## Offline + language
- 46.2% MSMEs fully offline; among non-users ~46% say they don't NEED digital (perception, not connectivity)
- 97.3% unaware of any government digitalisation scheme
- 98% of 870M internet users access Indic content; 57% urban prefer Indic — but training is "generic, English-centric"

## Integrity notes
- Meta/YouGov has no India-only breakout; ISF/Meta is a convenience sample with two internal inconsistencies — both flagged.
- Language as driver of offlineness is inferential, not yet quantified — flagged.
