## Thesis
The Intelligence Inversion: AI makes human intellect so cheap and abundant that the economic order — work, money, meaning — cracks apart within ~1,000 days. Humans' economic role shifts to owning, verifying, and caring.

## Key claims (with dates)
- Any screen-based job gets absorbed by AI "better, faster, cheaper" (2026).
- Three futures: Digital Feudalism / Great Fragmentation / open decentralised intelligence as public infrastructure (2026).
- Open-source AI is a stability requirement against centralised Oracle-AI feudalism (2026).
- Universal access to intelligence is the baseline of a viable new social contract (2026).
- A "second economy" emerges — machine-speed, agent-to-agent (2026).
