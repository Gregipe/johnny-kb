## Thesis
Scarce, controllable positions of economic power ("the Heights") durably capture the gains of increasingly capable and diffused intelligence. Intelligence commoditises; value agglomerates to distribution and network owners; most firms become coal for the furnace.

## Key claims (with dates)
- Intelligence is the giveaway; margins derive from being the conduit (2026).
- Hyperscaler capex ~$800B 2026, >$1T next; training cost +3.5x/yr; cost per task -47%/quarter (Epoch, Sep 2026).
- India: 16 net new tech jobs per 100 graduates (Nasscom FY2026); TCS headcount -23,460, operating profit +7.5% (FY2026).
- Fourth firm settlement: few operators paid like capital directing models.
- Licensed/liable humans retain value when intelligence is abundant (the Guild); youth hiring frozen (Stanford: -19% for 22-25yos in AI-exposed roles, mid-2026).
- Neo-Dollar: stablecoin flows on messaging rails; toll-able transactions.
- "Sell your Heights while they remain so."
