**Title:** India jobs & joblessness — fresher/graduate crisis
**Date:** 2026-11-05 (requested via Greg voice note)

## Thesis
The official 5% unemployment headline hides a structural graduate crisis: jobs exist but don't absorb freshers; the entry-level cognitive rung is being automated out first. This is the Intelligence Inversion showing up in Indian data.

## Key claims (with dates)
- Graduate unemployment: 19.2% male / 29.4% female (PLFS 2025).
- ~85% engineering graduates unplaced in 2026 despite hiring (talent report).
- 16 net tech jobs per 100 Indian graduates (Nasscom FY2026).
- TCS: headcount -23,460 while operating profit +7.5% (FY2026) — profit/headcount decoupling.
- Young workers frozen out, not fired: -19% employment for 22–25yos in AI-exposed roles (Stanford, mid-2026).
