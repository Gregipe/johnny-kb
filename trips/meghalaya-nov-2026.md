- 10:55 — AI2952, seat 13A, Mumbai T2 → Delhi T3 (arr 13:15). Ask at Mumbai counter to check bags through to Guwahati.

- Note: Elephant Falls impossible today (lands 17:05, sunset 4:45) — moved to Day 3 morning.

## Day 2 — Sun Nov 8: Mawphlang Sacred Grove + David Scott Trail

- 06:30 — Breakfast at Vivanta. Pack water, trail snacks, light rain shell.
- 07:15 — Leave for Mawphlang (25 km, ~45–50 min).
- 08:00 — Sacred Grove. Guide is MANDATORY (booked at counter, ~₹350 + ₹20–30/person entry). Walk ~2–2.5h: monoliths, sacrifice sites, Khasi folklore. Rules: nothing — not a leaf — leaves the grove. Same guide continues to the trail.
- 10:30 — Start David Scott Trail. Full route Mawphlang→Lad Mawphlang is 16 km / 4.5–6h — NOT realistic with the grove + a kid in daylight. Recommended: guided partial — Mawphlang to the river crossing / mid-valley and back (~8–10 km round, ~3h), returning by 2 PM. Full-trail option only if Dave is a strong walker and you start the trail by 10:15 sharp.
- 15:00 — Drive back to Shillong (~45 min). Rest.
- Evening — light dinner, early night. (Guide for the day ~₹1,000–1,500 total incl. trail; carry cash.)

## Day 3 — Mon Nov 9: Shillong → Sohra (Cherrapunji)

- 08:30 — Check out Vivanta.
- 09:00 — Elephant Falls (in Shillong, 15 min from hotel; ~45 min visit, open 9–5).
- 10:00 — Drive to Sohra (~54 km). Stops on route:
  - Mawkdok Valley viewpoint (~11:00): cliff-edge valley views; zipline for Dave if keen (₹700–1,000).
  - Arwah Cave (~12:30): lit limestone walk, ~45–60 min; site guide ~₹200.
  - Nohkalikai Falls (~14:00): India's tallest plunge fall, viewpoint 20–30 min (flows well in early Nov).
- 15:00 — Check in Polo Orchid Resort, Mawsmai-Nongthymmai. Evening at resort.
- Note: packed lunch or eat at local spots on the Sohra road; limited evening options outside the resort.

## Day 4 — Tue Nov 10: Nongriat Double-Decker Living Root Bridge

- 07:30 — Breakfast at Polo Orchid. Carry 2L water pp, snacks, cash (village contributions).
- 08:30 — Drive to Tyrna village (~15–20 km, ~45 min).
- 09:15 — Start trek (guide available at Tyrna, ~₹500–1,000): ~3,000 steps down, three suspension bridges, ~1.5–2h to Nongriat.
- 11:30 — Double-Decker Living Root Bridge + blue pool. Simple lunch at Nongriat homestay (~₹200–300 thali).
- 13:00 — Rainbow Falls ONLY if energy + weather allow (+1.5h return).
- 13:30–16:00 — Return climb (steeper, 2.5–3h). Must clear the forest by 4:30.
- 16:30 — Drive back to Polo Orchid. Rest; early dinner.
- Honest flag: with Dave this is the hardest day of the trip. If he flags, skip Rainbow and pace the return.

## Day 5 — Wed Nov 11: Sohra recovery loop

- 09:00 — Leisurely start. Mawsmai Cave (walking distance from Polo Orchid): lit limestone cave, 30–45 min.
- 10:30 — Dainthlen Falls (nearby): viewpoint ~15 min.
- 11:15 — Seven Sisters Falls viewpoint (Eco Park / Nohsngithiang): ~20–30 min.
- 12:15 — Kynrem Falls at Thangkharang Park (~12 km south, ~1.5h round trip): one of the tallest in the state, flows well early Nov.
- 14:30 — Back to Polo Orchid. Afternoon rest; pack for tomorrow (night-bag only for Shnongpdeng).

## Day 6 — Thu Nov 12: Bamboo Trail → Shnongpdeng

- 08:00 — Check out Polo Orchid (keep main luggage in the cab; pack one small bag each for Shnongpdeng — Gawooh is 8 min on foot past a suspension bridge).
- 09:30 — Reach Wahkhen (~1.5h). Mawryngkhang Bamboo Trail start: bamboo walkways hugging cliff faces and ravines ("scariest trek in Meghalaya"). Guide from Wahkhen ~₹500–800. Full route 4–5h round trip; first section (bamboo bridge + King's Way) is the highlight. Parent judgment for Dave on the cliff ledges — many families do it, but respect heights.
- 14:30 — Finish trail. Drive Wahkhen → Shnongpdeng (~2.5h via Pynursla).
- 16:30 — PITSTOP: Dawki suspension bridge — the iconic steel bridge over the Umngot, golden-light photo stop (10–15 min, zero detour; it's on the road between Dawki town and Shnongpdeng).
- 17:00 — Arrive Gawooh Adventure: entry ₹50/pp, SUV parking ₹100/night. Cross suspension bridge, 8-min walk with night-bags. Deluxe cabin ₹4,650 incl. breakfast.
- At check-in — BOOK Amkoi boat + guide with Gawooh for tomorrow 7 AM (they run the Sliang–Amkoi treks; life jackets provided; ask for an early boat before the 10 AM activity window).
- Evening — homemade thali (veg ₹250 / non-veg ₹350), bonfire by the Umngot.

## Day 7 — Fri Nov 13: Amkoi → Mawlynnong → Shillong

- 06:30 — Early breakfast at Gawooh. Day-packs only; wear grippy shoes (riverbed boulders can be slick); life jackets for the boat.
- 07:00 — Amkoi expedition (guide + boat arranged through Gawooh the night before): boat upstream on the Umngot ~30 min → riverbed/forest trail ~30–45 min (moderate, boulder sections) → Amkoi falls: white-rock pools, swimming, snorkelling/cliff jumping optional (November water is crisp). ~2–2.5h at the falls, then return boat. Back by ~12:30.
- 12:30 — Quick lunch in Shnongpdeng (thali at Gawooh before leaving).
- 13:15 — Drive to Mawlynnong (~1h). PITSTOP en route: Tamabil border viewpoint + market (~15 min) — India–Bangladesh border gate, small market of Bangladeshi chocolates/pickles/curios; Dave will love the one-road-two-countries border. (If the Dawki bridge was missed on day 6, grab it here too.)
- 14:15 — Mawlynnong: village walk (Asia's cleanest village, 30–40 min) + Riwai Living Root Bridge (15 min walk). Snack stop instead of full lunch.
- 15:00 — Leave Mawlynnong. Drive to Shillong (~3.5h via Pynursla). Laitlum CUT (can't beat sunset). Phe Phe Falls CUT — Amkoi is the better water experience and covers it.
- 18:30 — Check in Vivanta. Farewell dinner in Shillong.

- 11:00 — Check out Vivanta. 11:30 latest departure for Guwahati (~4–4.5h with one break).
- 16:00 — Arrive GAU T2. Akasa counters close 16:25 — don't push it.
